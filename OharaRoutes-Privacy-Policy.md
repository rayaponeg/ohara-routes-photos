# Ohara Routes Pin Manager – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

Ohara Routes Pin Manager is a private tool used only by the owner of the Ohara Routes business account to create and organize Pins and boards on that one Pinterest account. Each Pin links to an Ohara Routes product listing on Etsy.

## What the app accesses
- The Ohara Routes Pinterest account's own boards and Pins, so it can create Pins, add them to boards and avoid duplicates.
- Nothing from any other Pinterest user.

## What the app does not do
- It does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about anyone.
- It does not access other Pinners' accounts, Pins, boards or messages.
- It does not run ads or tracking.

## Data storage
Access to Pinterest is granted through Pinterest's own sign-in (OAuth). The access token is stored encrypted by the connector service and is used only to act on the Ohara Routes account. Access can be removed at any time from Pinterest settings.

## Contact
Questions about this policy can be sent through the Ohara Routes Etsy shop: https://www.etsy.com/shop/OharaRoutes
